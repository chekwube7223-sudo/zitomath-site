Support

Questions about ZitoMath, your subscription or your family's information? Email support@zitomath.com. We reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google: open your App Store or Google Play account settings to change or cancel ZitoMath Premium.

Privacy notice

Who we are

ZitoMath is a math learning app for children, operated by Digitalcian Technologies Limited, 10 Opaleye Street, Ajegunle, Lagos State, Nigeria (support@zitomath.com). Parents can contact us at any time about their child's information.

Who has an account

Only parents and guardians create accounts. Children never sign up, never sign in and never give us an email address. A parent adds each child as a profile with a nickname.

What we collect

From the parent: an email address (to sign in) and the date you gave consent. For each child: a nickname you choose, country and province, grade, which topics they've mastered (a score from 0 to 100) and minutes learned per day. A random ID for the device is used to apply fair daily limits.

What we never collect

Children's real names (unless you type one as the nickname), birthdays, photos, voice recordings, contacts, location, advertising IDs or any data for advertising. There are no ads, no tracking SDKs and no third-party analytics in ZitoMath.

How the AI tutor works

ZitoMath uses an AI system (Claude, by Anthropic) to write lessons and answer math questions. The AI is told only the grade, curriculum and topic. It is never sent the child's nickname, the parent's email or any account details. A child's typed questions are sent to answer them, but personal details such as emails, phone numbers and addresses are blocked by our server before they reach the AI. Tutor chats are not stored by ZitoMath. Children are told clearly that they are talking with an AI tutor, not a person.

Who we share with

We don't sell or rent data, and we don't share it for advertising. We use service providers only to run ZitoMath: Supabase (secure database and sign-in), Anthropic (the AI tutor), ElevenLabs (the optional premium narration voice, which only ever reads lesson text — never anything a child types) and RevenueCat with Apple/Google (subscriptions, linked to the parent account). Each may only use data to provide its service to us.

Parental consent

Before any child profile is created, the parent reads this notice and gives consent in the app. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account; we then stop collecting and delete your family's data.

Your choices and rights

In Settings (behind your parent PIN) you can see everything we hold (Download my data), change or delete any child's profile, and permanently delete your whole account and all your family's data. Deletion happens immediately on our servers. Depending on where you live (for example under COPPA and state privacy laws in the US, PIPEDA and provincial privacy laws in Canada, or the Nigeria Data Protection Act) you may have further rights, including complaining to your data protection regulator; contact us to use them.

Where data is stored

ZitoMath is operated from Nigeria. Families' data is stored on Supabase servers in the United States, and the AI and voice providers process requests in the United States. Each provider is bound by data-processing terms that require them to protect the data and use it only to provide their service. Where the law of your country (including the Nigeria Data Protection Act, Canadian privacy law) requires safeguards for international transfers, we rely on those contractual protections.

Person in charge of privacy

The person in charge of protecting personal information at ZitoMath is the Director of Digitalcian Technologies Limited. Contact them at support@zitomath.com for any question, request or complaint about your family's information. We reply within 30 days.

How long we keep data

Family data is kept while your account is active. If a family doesn't use ZitoMath for 12 months, the account and all children's data are deleted automatically. Daily usage counters are deleted after 35 days. Lesson content that isn't about any person (lessons, practice questions, narration audio) is kept to serve all learners.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each family's data is protected by row-level security so one family can never see another's. The parent area of the app is protected by a parent PIN. We follow a written information security program.

Changes

If we change how we handle children's data, we'll tell parents in the app and ask for consent again before the change applies.

Delete your account

In the app: open Settings (enter your parent PIN) and tap Delete account. Your account and all your children's data are deleted from our servers immediately, and from backups within 7 days.

Without the app: email support@zitomath.com from the email address you sign in with, asking us to delete your account. We'll confirm when it's done, within 30 days.

Deleting the account doesn't cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Data retention policy

Why we keep each item

Parent email: to sign in and contact you about your account. Consent date: to show consent was given. Child nickname, country, province and grade: to show the right curriculum. Mastery per topic and minutes per day: to choose the next lesson and show progress to you.

How long

While the account is active. After 12 months without use, the account and all its children's data are deleted automatically. Daily usage counters: 35 days. Tutor chats and practice answers: not stored. Server logs: no personal data.

Deletion

Parents can delete a child's profile or the whole account in the app at any time; it's removed from our servers immediately and from backups within the backup window (7 days).

Information security program

Responsible person

Digitalcian Technologies Limited designates a person responsible for coordinating this program, reviewing it at least once a year and after any incident.

Risk assessment

At least annually we review risks to children's information: unauthorised access to accounts, leaked keys, vendor breaches, and AI misuse. Findings and fixes are recorded.

Safeguards

Minimal data by design. Secrets (AI, voice and payment keys) live only on the server, never in the app. Database access uses row-level security. The service role key is used only inside server functions. Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Parent PIN in the app. Rate limits and daily caps on every AI and voice endpoint. Personal details are filtered from chat before reaching the AI. No child data is sent to the AI beyond the curriculum and the question itself.

Vendors

Supabase, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, RevenueCat, Apple and Google are used under their data-processing terms. We check that each keeps data secure and uses it only to provide the service.

Testing and monitoring

Automated tests cover the safety rules (no names to the AI, personal-info filter, topic validation, limits). Server logs contain no personal data. Access to the production database is limited to the responsible person.

Incidents

If children's data may have been exposed, we contain it, assess it, notify affected parents and regulators as the law requires, and update this program.

Cross-border transfer assessment

What is transferred

Parent email and consent date; for each child a nickname, country, province, grade, a mastery score per topic and minutes learned per day. Typed tutor questions are sent to the AI provider only to answer them, with personal details filtered out first, and are not stored. No real names, birthdays, photos, voice, location or advertising data are collected.

Where it goes and why

Supabase (database and sign-in, United States) to run accounts and sync; Anthropic (AI tutor, United States) to write lessons and answer questions, receiving only the curriculum, grade, topic and the question itself; ElevenLabs (United States) receives lesson text only; RevenueCat, Apple and Google handle subscriptions linked to the parent account.

Sensitivity

The data concerns children, so it is treated as sensitive even though it is limited to learning progress. It contains no health, financial, biometric or location information.

Protections in place

Each provider is bound by data-processing terms limiting use to providing its service. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Row-level security separates every family. Secrets stay on the server. Personal details are filtered before the AI. Families inactive for 12 months are deleted automatically, and parents can export or delete everything in the app at any time.

Legal framework at the destination

United States law gives fewer general privacy rights than Canadian law, but the providers' contractual commitments, their security certifications and the small amount of data involved reduce the risk. US authorities could in principle request data from providers; the data involved has little value for that purpose.

Conclusion

Given the limited data, its purpose and the protections above, the information will receive adequate protection, consistent with generally recognised principles of personal information protection. Digitalcian Technologies Limited reviews this assessment each year and whenever a provider or the data collected changes.
