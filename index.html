How it works

Set up in two minutes.
Learning in three.

  1. A parent creates the account

    Sign in with your email, give consent and set a parent PIN. Your child never needs an email or password.

  2. Pick the country and grade

    ZitoMath loads your child's curriculum: Common Core in the US, or your province's math curriculum in Canada.

  3. Learn a topic a day

    Watch a short animated lesson, try 5 practice questions, and ask the tutor whenever something doesn't click.

Animated lessons

See the math, not just the answer.

Each lesson is a short animated explainer narrated out loud: balance scales for equations, fraction pies, number lines, coins, graphs and more.

  • Step by step, at your child's pacePause, replay or skip any step. Lesson notes sit underneath for reading back.
  • Every number is calculated by the appThe pictures are drawn by ZitoMath itself, so the numbers on screen are always exactly right.
Practice that teaches

Mistakes become the lesson.

Short sets of 5 questions, easiest first. A wrong answer gets a hint, and one tap explains exactly what went wrong and how to fix it.

  • A fresh mix every timeQuestions change between sessions, so children learn the method, not the answers.
  • Double-checked answersEvery question is solved twice, independently, before a child ever sees it.
AI tutor

A patient tutor, any time.

Stuck on homework at 9pm? Your child can ask the tutor to explain it more simply, show another example or give a real-life one.

  • Only talks about mathIt stays on the current topic and gently steers back if asked about anything else.
  • Clearly an AI, never a personIt says so on screen, and it never asks for personal details.
