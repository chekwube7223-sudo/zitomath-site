For parents & safety

Safe by design, not by setting.

ZitoMath is built for children, so privacy and safety are part of how it works, not options you have to find and switch on.

Parent account and PIN

Only parents and guardians create accounts. Settings, subscriptions and personal details are locked behind your parent PIN.

Children never sign in

Your child gets a nickname and a grade. No email, no password, no profile photo, no chat with other people.

No ads. No tracking.

There are no ads, no tracking SDKs and no analytics in ZitoMath. We never sell or share your family's information.

The AI doesn't know who they are

The AI only receives the curriculum, grade, topic and the question. Never your child's nickname or your email.

Personal details removed

If a child types an email, phone number, address, postcode, link or "my name is…", it's removed on our server before anything reaches the AI.

Delete everything, anytime

One tap in Settings deletes your account and all your children's data from our servers immediately. Inactive families are deleted after 12 months.

How the tutor behaves

An AI tutor with clear rules.

ZitoMath's tutor follows child-safety rules on every single reply.

Always an AI

It's labelled "AI tutor, not a person" and says so if asked.

Math only

Off-topic questions get a kind nudge back to the lesson.

No personal details

It never asks for names, school, address or photos, and reminds children not to share them.

Points to trusted adults

If a child says they're upset or unsafe, it tells them to talk to a parent, teacher or another trusted adult.
