For parents & safety

Safe by design,
not by setting.

ZitoMath is built for children, so privacy and safety are part of how it works, not options you have to find and switch on.

You're in charge. Your child just learns.

Settings, subscriptions and personal details sit behind your parent PIN. Read the full privacy notice.
