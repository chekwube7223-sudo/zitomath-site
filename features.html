Features

Everything a great math tutor does.
In your pocket.

ZitoMath teaches each topic, lets your child practice it, explains mistakes and tracks progress, all following their school curriculum.

Animated lessons

Short, narrated explainers that build each idea step by step, with a worked example and a "your turn" moment.

Read aloud

Every lesson can be read aloud using the phone's voice, or with a natural premium voice. Adjustable speed for younger learners.

Practice that explains

5-question sets, easiest first, with hints and a step-by-step explanation of any mistake.

AI tutor chat

Ask for a simpler explanation, another example or a real-life one. Math only, and always labelled as an AI.

Progress and streaks

Minutes learned each week, streaks, and mastery for every unit, plus a weekly report you can share.

Your curriculum

US Common Core or your Canadian province, with the right spelling, money and units.

Inside a lesson

Built like a great teacher explains it.

Every lesson follows the same proven shape, so children always know what comes next.

  • Why it mattersA real-life reason the topic is useful, at your child's age.
  • The big ideaThe concept in plain words, with a picture.
  • Steps3 to 5 short steps, each with its own visual.
  • Worked example, then your turnOne problem solved line by line, then one for your child to try.
  • Watch outThe most common mistake, and how to avoid it.
Accuracy

Checked twice before your child sees it.

Math has to be right. Every lesson and practice question goes through an independent check before it's used.

  • Solved again from scratchA separate AI check re-solves each worked example and question without seeing the answer. If the two disagree, it's thrown away.
  • Matched to the gradeContent that's too hard, too easy or off-topic is rejected.
  • Pictures drawn by the appThe numbers in every visual are calculated by ZitoMath's own code, never guessed.
Worked example
3x + 5 = 20
  1. Subtract 5 from both sides: 3x = 15
  2. Divide both sides by 3: x = 5
  3. Check: 3(5) + 5 = 20 ✓
