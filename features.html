Features

Everything a great tutor does.

ZitoMath teaches each topic, lets your child practice it, explains every mistake and tracks progress, all following their school curriculum.

Animated, narrated lessons

Short explainers that build each idea step by step: a real-life hook, the big idea, steps, a worked example and a "your turn" moment. Pause, replay or skip any step, with notes underneath for reading back.

Practice that explains

Five-question sets, easiest first, in a fresh mix every time. Younger children get multiple choice. A wrong answer gets a hint, and one tap gives a step-by-step explanation of the mistake.

AI tutor chat

"Explain it more simply." "Show me another example." "Give me a real-life one." Math only, always labelled as an AI.

Read aloud

Every lesson read aloud with the phone's voice, or a natural premium voice. Slower, normal or faster.

Progress & reports

Minutes each week, streaks, mastery per unit and a weekly report you can share with a teacher.

Your curriculum, exactly

US Common Core or your Canadian province, with the right spelling, money and units. Each topic shows its code, so you can match it to homework.
