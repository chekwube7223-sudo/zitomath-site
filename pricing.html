FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Which curricula does ZitoMath follow?

In the United States, the Common Core State Standards from Kindergarten to Grade 8, plus Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2, Precalculus, Statistics and Calculus. In Canada, the math curriculum for Ontario, BC and Yukon, Alberta, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut, Saskatchewan and Manitoba, and Atlantic Canada. Quebec is coming soon.

What ages is it for?

Kindergarten (about age 5) to Grade 12. Lessons are written for the age of each grade, and younger children get multiple-choice answers and a read-aloud voice.

Does my child need their own account or email?

No. A parent or guardian creates the account and adds each child with a nickname and grade. Children never sign in.

How do you make sure the math is right?

Every lesson and practice question is solved again from scratch by a separate check that doesn't see the answer, and anything that doesn't match is thrown away. The pictures are drawn by the app itself, so their numbers are exact. If you ever spot a mistake, email support@zitomath.com and we'll fix it for everyone.

Is the AI tutor safe for children?

It only talks about math, is always labelled as an AI, never asks for personal details and never gives links. It doesn't receive your child's name, and personal details typed into the chat are removed first. More about safety.

What's included in the free plan?

3 lessons and 10 tutor questions a day, practice with explanations, progress tracking, the phone's read-aloud voice and one child. No ads, ever.

How does the free trial work?

Premium starts with a 7-day free trial. You won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google, and you can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Can I use one subscription for several children?

Yes. Premium covers up to 5 children, each with their own grade, curriculum and report.

Does ZitoMath work offline?

Lessons your child has already opened can be replayed offline, and narration is saved on the phone. New lessons, practice and the tutor need a connection.

When can I download ZitoMath?

ZitoMath is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. The download buttons on this site go live on launch day.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings (enter your parent PIN) and tap Delete account. Everything is deleted from our servers immediately. See how to delete your account.
